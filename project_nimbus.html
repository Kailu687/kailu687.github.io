Project Nimbus

Smart India Hackathon 2025 Project

SIH 2025AvionicsHybrid VTOLConcept & design

Overview

Thrilled to present Project Nimbus, our entry for the Smart India Hackathon internal round at VIT Chennai! From 864 teams in Round 1, only 212 advanced — and we were proud to be one of them. Nimbus is a hybrid VTOL disaster‑relief drone designed to deliver medical supplies and communication devices to remote regions during earthquakes, floods, and other emergencies.

The Problem

Our challenge was to build:

  • A drone capable of operating in disaster zones
  • AI-based real-time navigation + obstacle avoidance
  • Payload capacity ≥ 5 kg
  • Reliable delivery to remote locations

Traditional drones either lack range (multirotors) or require runways (fixed‑wings).

Our solution??? Nimbus 007.

Nimbus solves this through a hybrid VTOL + fixed‑wing configuration.

These figures are design targets for the Nimbus 007 concept, not results measured on a flying aircraft.

App Interface

We developed a monitoring interface concept for mission status, alerts, and telemetry visualization.

Tilt Mechanism Animation

Here is the tilt-rotor transition mechanism visualized in motion. Nimbus 007 takes off like a conventional quadcopter, and the forward two rotors tilt 90 degrees so it can fly like a fixed-wing aircraft, improving speed and accuracy.

Structural Analysis

We planned to perform stress analysis and subsystem-level validation in FreeCAD. Due to computational limits, the full-scale simulations are planned to be split into modular studies.

Outcome & Reflection

We didn’t make it to the national top 50, but Nimbus 007 gave us real engineering exposure — avionics, CAD, simulation, teamwork, and rapid prototyping. Nimbus 007 doesn't end here. This is just the beginning of my aerospace journey.
