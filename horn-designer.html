Vircator Horn Designer

HPMAntennasAnsys HFSSResearch tool

A virtual cathode oscillator (vircator) radiates the TM01 mode, which has a null on boresight. This corrugated horn converts TM01 to TE11 inside its own grooves: the groove centres are offset sideways by δ·sin(2πz/ΛB). Enter your vircator's frequency and get the full design, scaled exactly from the HFSS-simulated 3.665 GHz horn.

Your vircator

Every length is multiplied by 3.665 / f. A metal horn obeys Maxwell's equations exactly under this scaling, so the simulated performance carries over unchanged.

      About the design
      
          
      The reference horn was simulated in Ansys HFSS 2025 R2 at 3.665 GHz. In the stand-alone converter, 75 % of the TM01 power converts to TE11, against 0.1 % without the groove offset. In the full horn fed with TM01, the on-axis realized gain is 13.65 dBi, against −13.87 dBi for the same horn without the offset. These are simulation results on a mesh that did not fully converge. No hardware has been built.
